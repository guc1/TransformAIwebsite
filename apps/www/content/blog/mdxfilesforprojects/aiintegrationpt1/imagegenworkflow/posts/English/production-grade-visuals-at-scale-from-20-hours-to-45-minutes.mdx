# Production-Grade Visuals at Scale: From 20 Hours to 45 Minutes

![blogpost](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/aiintegrationpt1/imagegenworkflow/images/imagegenwork.png)

AI image generation has matured to the point where, when done correctly, differences from human-made visuals can be nearly impossible to spot. That doesn’t mean anyone can consistently produce **production-grade** images on the first try. In practice, prompt skill matters: **experts average ~5 generations**, **intermediate users ~10**, and **beginners 20+** to get a “perfect” image.

Working with a partner, we identified that they could reduce costs on visual production by integrating AI image generation **and** editing. Off-the-shelf tools weren’t scalable enough and were too slow for their workload. Together, we built a custom platform that ran multiple generations in parallel, stored optimal prompts, and integrated the entire workflow with their design process.

## The Challenge
- High volumes of visuals needed for marketing and product work  
- Variable quality and speed depending on prompt skill (≈5 / 10 / 20+ generations)  
- Off-the-shelf “single run” pipelines lacked scalability and throughput

**Goal:** Achieve consistent, production-grade output with faster turnaround and lower manual effort.

## What We Built (with the Partner)
- **Parallel generation orchestration:** multiple generations per brief, automatically.  
- **Prompt memory & reuse:** a library that stores and replays the best prompts for similar tasks.  
- **Integrated editing workflow:** generation → edit → approval in one trackable flow.  

This combination addressed both **quality** (repeatability via saved prompts) and **speed** (parallelism + streamlined handoffs).

## Results (from the project)
Time to produce the **same quality** image:

| Workflow                         | Labor time |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional (manual)             | **20 hours** |
| “Normal” AI integration          | **7 hours** |
| **Custom platform (co-built)**   | **45 minutes** |

> We’re not sharing cost figures; they’re confidential. The partner reported meaningful reductions tied to the labor time improvements above.

## Why It Matters
When a company produces a lot of visuals, shaving hours off each asset compounds quickly—shorter cycles, more experimentation, and faster campaign launches.
