# AI Solutions for HR: Finding the Right People—Faster

![Cover image](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/aiintegrationpt1/hrfinderai/images/hrcoverim.png)

Finding the right people for the right job is critical. For AI-related requests, companies turn to TransformAI. One client came to us with a clear challenge: **build an AI-powered recruiter that could take a task and surface all eligible candidates for a role**. They didn’t care about costs—they wanted performance. And that’s exactly what they got.

## The Challenge
Traditional HR agencies can deliver strong matches, but they’re often expensive and time-intensive. Our client wanted a system that:
- Rapidly identifies qualified candidates for specific roles
- Scales beyond manual research
- Produces a more complete candidate list
- Ultimately lowers hiring costs through efficiency

## The AI-Powered HR Researcher
We designed an HR research assistant that ingests a role brief (requirements, responsibilities, preferences) and then executes a structured search for relevant profiles. The assistant compiles potential candidates and organizes them for downstream review.

**What it does (at a glance):**
- Interprets job criteria from a task/brief  
- Searches for matching candidates  
- Prioritizes and aggregates results for hiring teams  

> Note: This post summarizes outcomes from one client project and includes only the claims provided.

## Results
The client’s core objective was speed and coverage. The AI HR researcher delivered:

| Metric / Outcome                        | Result (from client project) |
| --- | --- |
| Rate of finding suitable candidates     | **+1200%** (i.e., **12× faster**) |
| Coverage of potential candidates        | **More complete lists** (qualitatively reported) |
| Hiring costs                            | **Reduced** via faster sourcing and better coverage |
