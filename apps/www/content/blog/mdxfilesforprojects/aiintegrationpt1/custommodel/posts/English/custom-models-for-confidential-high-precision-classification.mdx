# Custom Models for Confidential, High-Precision Classification

![Cover: conceptual on-prem custom model for sensitive classification](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/aiintegrationpt1/custommodel/images/covermodel.png)

Large language models like ChatGPT excel at language-heavy tasks—but some use cases demand **strict output formats**, **tight cost controls**, and **confidential handling**. In those scenarios, prompting alone isn’t enough (or becomes too expensive). With a partner, we identified a high-impact opportunity to **classify incoming requests** with AI, cutting labor while maintaining quality.

## Why a Custom Model (Instead of Prompting)?
- **Format rigidity:** inexpensive LLMs **struggled to emit the exact format** required.  
- **Confidential data:** sensitive content made third-party processing undesirable.  
- **Economics:** calculations suggested **>95% cost savings** with automation, and a **test case study** indicated quality would improve.

## The Solution: Train and Deploy On-Prem
We trained a **custom model** on the partner’s data and formatting rules and **deployed it on local hardware**. This kept data in-house and stabilized unit economics.

- **Task focus:** detect **mistakes in user requests** that can signal **potential fraud** (for routing/escalation).  
- **Output compliance:** schema- and format-aware generation.  
- **Privacy by design:** **on-prem** inference; no external calls.

> Claim recap from the project sketch: **>95% calculated cost savings**; **quality increase** based on a **test case study**.

## Case Study: Human vs AI (Correctness & Effort)
We evaluated performance and effort on the same request set:

| System | Correctness | Human labor time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Human reviewers** | **80%** | **24 hours** |
| **Custom AI (on-prem)** | **79%** | **1 hour** |

**Interpretation:** The **AI achieved near-human correctness (79% vs 80%)** with dramatically lower effort (1h vs 24h), while satisfying strict formatting and confidentiality constraints.

## Implementation Notes
- **Data prep & eval:** fully in-environment; emphasis on format adherence and mistake detection relevant to **potential fraud**.  
- **Operations:** local serving for predictable latency and cost; iterations focused on schema fidelity and controlling false positives.

## TL;DR
When formats are strict, data is confidential, and costs matter, a **custom on-prem model** can outperform prompt-only setups. In our test, AI reached **79% vs 80% human** with **1h vs 24h** of effort and **>95%** calculated cost savings.
