# AI Email Workflows: From Best Tool? to the Right Solution

Companies often ask us which email responder is “best.” In practice, the answer depends on specific workflows and requirements—and that’s where off-the-shelf tools frequently fall short. For teams that needed capabilities beyond standard products, we built custom AI email solutions that delivered strong gains in both efficiency and outcomes.

## The Gap in Off-the-Shelf Responders
Many teams evaluated popular responders but found key features missing, which meant they couldn’t use them—or weren’t allowed to, due to compliance and process constraints. The problem wasn’t the concept of AI email; it was fit.

**Common blockers we encountered:**
- Missing features tied to approval flows or CRM syncing
- Role- and team-specific rules for tone, content, or legal phrasing
- Performance/scalability limits for high-volume inboxes

## What We Built (Custom, for Specific Needs)
In response, we delivered custom AI email workflows tailored to each company’s policies and stack. These systems were relatively complex under the hood (routing, rules, learning loops), but easy to use in daily work.

**Observed outcomes from these projects:**
- **Time spent on email decreased by over 75%** (per day, on average)
- **Response rates increased by over 50%**

> Note: Results reflect client-reported figures from the projects we delivered.

**Why this works:** The AI focuses on triage, draft generation, context recall, and structured follow-ups—while humans make the final call on sensitive messages.

## Quick Summary of Results
| Metric                               | Outcome (reported) |
| --- | --- |
| Time spent on email (daily average)  | **↓ over 75%** |
| Response rate on outgoing emails     | **↑ over 50%** |

## What’s Next: A More Universal Solution
Not every team is ready to invest in a fully custom build. Based on repeated requests, we’re developing a **more all-round, universal solution** that incorporates what we’ve learned—aimed at broader compatibility and easier adoption. **Development is ongoing.**

In the meantime, we continue to offer **custom solutions** for teams with specific requirements. For many companies, **automating email workflows is one of the most obvious and powerful AI integrations** they can make.

Off-the-shelf responders often miss critical features. Our **custom AI email workflows** helped clients **reduce daily email time by 75%+** and **lift response rates by 50%+**. We’re also building a **more universal solution** informed by these projects to make adoption easier for more teams.
