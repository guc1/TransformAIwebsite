# Why We Built Our Education App (and What It Changed)

As our education services expanded, we hit a quality ceiling. In an educational setting, we constantly chased the most value from AI models but ran into practical limits: we couldn’t evaluate every single output at scale, budgets capped how many models/accounts we could use (opening more than five accounts per provider isn’t viable for most companies), and—most importantly—we felt there was far more potential to unlock. After researching the market and finding no application that met our needs, we did the rational thing: we built our own app. *(Rational companies might have stopped at the thought—but we pushed through.)*

![Cover image](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/education/educationapp/images/covereduapp.png)

## The Bottlenecks We Hit
- **Evaluation capacity:** We couldn’t meaningfully review every participant output at scale.  
- **Model access vs. budgets:** Using all the models we wanted wasn’t feasible; **>5 accounts per provider** isn’t viable for most companies.  
- **Untapped potential:** We knew the pedagogy and the tech could deliver more than our tooling allowed.

> Conclusion: our constraints were tool- and process-related, not motivation- or demand-related.

## Building the Platform (Yes, We Actually Did It)
We built an education platform tailored to AI learning at scale. The goal: keep quality high while removing the operational choke points.

- **Custom modules:** The platform lets us design **custom modules** that leverage AI to help people get **better results faster** in an **interactive** way.  
- **Scalable evaluation:** The app architecture supports reviewing outputs at volume without sacrificing standards.  
- **Cost efficiency via APIs:** We pay for **what we use, token-wise**, which keeps costs aligned with usage rather than fixed per-seat fees.


## Results So Far (What Users Report)
From surveys of groups who used both our old workshop-style approach and the new app, reported **learning experience** increased from **6.1/10** to **9.2/10**.

**At a glance**

| Dimension               | Before (old method) | Now (our app)      |
|------------------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Learning experience    | 6.1 / 10            | 9.2 / 10           |
| Output evaluation      | Hard to scale       | Designed to scale  |
| Model access           | Budget-limited      | API pay-per-token  |
| Learning approach      | Static exercises    | Interactive modules |

## Why This Matters (And What’s Next)
- **Bigger scale, better results:** We can educate more people **and** improve outcomes compared to pre-platform days.  
- **Fit to real work:** Custom modules match how people actually use AI—hands-on and iterative.  
- **Ongoing upgrades:** Things are never finished; we’re actively updating the application so the experience keeps improving. The current version is already a significant step forward from traditional exercises—what’s coming next should be **super transformative**.

## TL;DR
We hit quality and scale limits with our old approach (evaluation capacity, model/account constraints, and unrealized potential). We built an **education platform** that uses **custom modules**, **scalable evaluation**, and **API-based costs**. In surveys of users who tried both methods, the **learning experience improved from 6.1/10 to 9.2/10**. The present is better—and the roadmap is aimed at making it even more transformative.
