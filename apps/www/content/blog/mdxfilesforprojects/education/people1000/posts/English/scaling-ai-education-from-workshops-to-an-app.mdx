# Scaling AI Education: From Workshops to an App

Since early 2025, our education services for companies have **improved AI skills for 1,000+ people**—specifically their ability to build **custom solutions** with AI tools and to **explore new custom workflows** that boost performance. We began with in-person workshops, but as group sizes grew, maintaining our quality became difficult. That’s when we introduced our **education app**, a state-of-the-art application that lets us **scale** while keeping standards high.

![Cover image](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/education/people1000/images/thousendpeople.png)

## Why Workshops Didn’t Scale for Us
- **Growing groups, harder quality control:** As groups got larger, it became harder to ensure consistent guidance and depth.
- **Limited iteration speed:** Workshop formats made it difficult to rapidly adapt tasks to different skill levels.
- **Bottlenecked feedback:** Instructor time was the constraint; personalized feedback couldn’t reach everyone at the right moment.

> We found that beyond a certain group size, our workshop model made it hard to guarantee the quality we expect.

## What Changed with the Education App
Our **education app** was built to preserve quality while scaling delivery.

- **Group-tuned modules:** Each session uses a short briefing, a clear assignment, and hands-on work with curated materials.
- **Built-in guidance:** The app structures practice and supports participants as they create outputs.
- **Consistent evaluation:** A repeatable flow—peer ranking plus structured review—keeps standards consistent as groups grow.

**Then vs. Now (at a glance)**

| Aspect               | Workshops (early days)              | Education app (now)                      |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Group size handling  | Quality drops as size increases     | Designed to scale while keeping quality  |
| Personalization      | Harder to adjust on the fly         | Modules tuned to group needs             |
| Feedback loop        | Instructor bottleneck               | Structured peer + guided review          |

## Impact Since Early 2025 (What We Did)
- **1,000+ people:** We improved participants’ **AI skills** and their **ability to create custom solutions** using AI tools.  
- **Workflow exploration:** Participants gained the skills to **explore and design new custom workflows** that improve their AI performance.  
- **Quality at scale:** The app helped us **teach more people** without compromising our standards.


## TL;DR
Starting in **early 2025**, we moved from **workshops**—which became hard to keep high-quality at larger sizes—to a **state-of-the-art education app**. This shift helped us **improve AI skills for 1,000+ people**, including building **custom AI solutions** and **exploring new workflows**, while maintaining quality as we scale.
