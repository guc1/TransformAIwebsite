# Custom AI Learning Modules with Built-In Co-Teachers

Inside our custom education app, we design **custom modules** per session and per cohort. Because beginners and advanced users have different needs, a universal module doesn’t work. Our approach is **practice-first**: a concise briefing, a clear assignment, and curated sources—then learners generate outputs, rank results, and receive targeted feedback from both a human **Module Leader** and **built-in AIs**. These AIs assist teaching during the module and **generate improvement reports** for each participant.

![Cover image](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/education/modules/images/custommodules.png)

## Why one-size-fits-all fails—and how AI co-teaches
- **Cohort-matched challenge:** Modules are tuned to the group’s level instead of one generic track.  
- **Practice-first:** Short info → assignment → hands-on generation.  
- **AI as co-teacher:** Built-in AIs guide participants during tasks (nudges, checks, clarifications) and help the Module Leader surface patterns worth discussing.  
- **Evidence-driven:** Outputs are ranked by peers, then reviewed by the Module Leader **and** the AIs.

## How a module runs (with AI checkpoints)
1. **Briefing:** A focused info block sets goals and criteria.  
2. **Assignment & materials:** Clear task + sources and tools provided inside the app.  
3. **Generation:** Participants produce outputs (e.g., text, images, prompts, workflows).  
4. **Peer ranking:** A built-in ranking system lets participants evaluate outputs from the group.  
5. **Dual evaluation:** Top outputs are evaluated by the **Module Leader** and by **custom built-in AIs**.  
6. **Tips & iteration:** Cohort-wide tips and tricks are shared to improve next attempts.  
7. **AI improvement reports:** Each participant receives an AI-generated report with concrete suggestions tailored to their submission.

**At-a-glance module flow**

| Stage       | What happens                            | AI’s role                              |
|-------------|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Briefing    | Short context + target                   | Surfaces key criteria inside the UI    |
| Assignment  | Clear task + materials                   | Suggests relevant sources/checks       |
| Generation  | Participants create outputs              | Inline hints and formative feedback    |
| Ranking     | Peers evaluate outputs                   | Detects patterns and outliers          |
| Evaluation  | Leader + AIs review top work             | Structured, criteria-based notes       |
| Tips        | Group guidance                           | Synthesizes common improvement areas   |
| Reports     | Individual feedback                      | Tailored improvement reports           |

## What the built-in AIs do
- **Assist during practice:** Provide nudges, sanity checks, and quick clarifications without derailing hands-on work.  
- **Support evaluation:** Apply consistent criteria to highlight strengths and gaps in top-ranked outputs.  
- **Generate reports:** Produce **participant-specific reports** that summarize performance and list **actionable next steps**.  
- **Surface cohort insights:** Help the Module Leader spot recurring issues to address in the tips & tricks segment.

## TL;DR
Custom, practice-first modules—brief info, clear assignments, and curated materials—are followed by **peer ranking** and **dual evaluation** (Module Leader + built-in AIs). The AIs **co-teach** during exercises and **generate personalized improvement reports**, turning real outputs into actionable learning for both focused skills and advanced, interdisciplinary AI use.
