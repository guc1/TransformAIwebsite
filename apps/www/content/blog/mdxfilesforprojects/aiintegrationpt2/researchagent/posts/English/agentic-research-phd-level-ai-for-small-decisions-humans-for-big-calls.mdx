# Agentic Research: PhD-Level AI for Small Decisions, Humans for Big Calls

![Cover: researcher guiding AI agents in a human-in-the-loop workflow](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/aiintegrationpt2/researchagent/images/researchcoverim.png)

Researchers are expensive—and too often, their time goes to **gathering data** instead of **analyzing it**. While we help other companies transform with AI, a core pillar of TransformAI is our **research department**. We built an **internal, agentic workflow** so that AI automates repetitive tasks and first-pass analysis, while researchers focus on interpretation and strategy.

> Some AI models already demonstrate **PhD-level reasoning** on many routine decisions. We let them make those **small decisions**—but we keep **humans in the loop** for the bigger ones, for reasons of **safety, quality control, and regulation**.

## The Challenge
- Manual data collection is repetitive, slow, and costly.  
- Researchers create the most value by **interpreting results** and **forming theories**, not by copy/pasting.  
- We needed a system where **AI does the legwork** and **humans steer direction**.

## Our Agentic Workflow (Human-in-the-Loop)
- **Scope:** collect data on companies’ AI integrations via **public web research** and **respectful, compliant outreach**.  
- **Agents:** autonomous routines that run under **researcher guidance** (goals, constraints, quality thresholds).  
- **Normalization:** clean, tag, and structure data for downstream analysis—ready for researcher review.  

**Impact (reported):** the workflow **saves ~10 minutes of human labor per data point**. This matters at scale—we are **collecting millions of data points** to enable more robust analyses.

## Governance: Humans Own the Big Calls
- **Safety & quality control:** researchers approve methods, review outputs, and gate deployment.  
- **Regulations & compliance:** public information and lawful outreach only; logs ensure **auditability** and **reproducibility**.  
- **Future-ready:** as agents improve, the workflow becomes **more autonomous**, giving researchers **more leverage**—without removing human oversight where it counts.

## Why It Matters
By letting AI handle the repetitive work and **small decisions**, we free researchers to do what only they can: **interpret, theorize, and guide**. That shift compounds at scale—shorter cycles, richer datasets, and stronger findings.

## TL;DR
We run a **human-in-the-loop, agentic research workflow**: AI makes **small decisions** and automates repetitive tasks (**~10 minutes saved per data point**), while **humans make the big calls** for safety, quality, and compliance—as we scale to **millions** of data points.
