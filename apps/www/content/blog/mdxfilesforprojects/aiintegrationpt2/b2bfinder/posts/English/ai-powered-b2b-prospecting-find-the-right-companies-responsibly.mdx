# AI-Powered B2B Prospecting: Find the Right Companies—Responsibly

![Cover: AI-assisted B2B discovery and compliant outreach](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/aiintegrationpt2/b2bfinder/images/b2bcoverim.png)

In B2B, the hardest part isn’t writing the pitch—it’s finding **the right target group** and a **legitimate way to reach them**. Doing this manually either costs a lot of time or a lot of money. To close that gap, we built an **AI agent** that searches the public web to identify companies matching your criteria and returns **publicly available** ways to contact them—accurately, efficiently, and with clear guardrails.

## The Problem: Precision Prospecting Is Expensive
Great B2B sales rely on great lists. But:
- Manual research is slow and variable in quality.
- Purchased lists can be expensive, generic, or outdated.
- You still need to ensure **compliance** and **legality** when reaching out.

**Goal:** Automate “find the right businesses + public contact routes” while staying within ethical and legal boundaries.

## The Solution: An Agent Trained for Web-Scale Discovery
Our agent uses **tool calling** to navigate the open web (search and site traversal), extract **publicly available** company details, and compile **legitimate, public contact options**.

**How it works (high level):**
- **Requirement parsing:** interpret your criteria (industry, size, tech stack, geography).
- **Targeted web search:** query, follow, and validate sources.
- **Signal-based filtering:** prioritize companies that match specified attributes.
- **Public contact routes only:** surface contact pages, generic inboxes, and official profiles.
- **Structured output:** lists ready for compliant outreach workflows.

> We trained the agent specifically on *how* to tackle B2B discovery queries for **accuracy** and **efficiency**—not for scraping gated data or bypassing terms.

## Ethics & Compliance by Design
We’ve provided this capability to partners **with explicit instructions**: do **not** use it for harmful or illegal purposes. AI is powerful; **just because something is possible, doesn’t mean it should be integrated**. We focus on:
- **Public information only** (no circumvention, no private data).
- **Lawful bases for contact** (respect local regulations and platform policies).
- **Respectful outreach** (frequency caps, opt-outs, data minimization).

## Where It Helps
- **Account-based marketing (ABM):** higher-fidelity target lists from public signals.
- **Market entry scans:** quickly map a niche to identify relevant players.
- **Partnership sourcing:** find complementary companies and the right public routes to start a conversation.

> We emphasize **fit over volume**: the agent aims to reduce time-to-fit, not to maximize raw lead counts.

## TL;DR
Manual B2B prospecting is slow and costly. Our **AI prospecting agent** uses tool calling to search the public web, match companies to your requirements, and return **public, legitimate contact routes**—with **clear ethical guardrails**. Used responsibly, it creates real value for **both** businesses in B2B.
