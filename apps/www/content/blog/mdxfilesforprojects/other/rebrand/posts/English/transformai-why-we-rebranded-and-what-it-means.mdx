# TransformAI: Why We Rebranded (and What It Means)

*Transformations are our business.* As we expanded from niche AI integrations to full-scale AI transformations, we realized our former name—**StaccatoAI**—no longer represented who we are. It was time to do what we help our clients do: **transform**.

![Cover image placeholder](/images/blog-images/mdxfilesforprojects/other/rebrand/images/staccatototransform.png)

## Why the name change

Our work has evolved: from implementing specific AI features to guiding **end-to-end AI transformations** inside organizations. The new name, **TransformAI**, says exactly what we do—**AI transformations**—in one clear word.

- **Clarity of focus:** When people hear “TransformAI,” we want them to immediately associate it with AI transformations—our core business.
- **Built for our market:** We’re mainly focused on the Dutch market, and *transform* works in **both Dutch and English**, which supports future expansion across Europe.
- **Philosophy in a name:** We believe you should **transform AI into your business**, not let AI transform your business.

> Take control of AI—make it serve your strategy, not the other way around.

## Why “TransformAI”

Beyond clarity, the name reflects how we work with clients:

1. **From point solutions to full programs:** We design transformations across processes, teams, and tech—so AI sticks and scales.  
2. **Language that travels:** A single, multilingual word keeps our positioning coherent in the Netherlands and beyond.  
3. **Identity that fits the mission:** The brand aligns with our everyday work—helping organizations *become* AI-enabled, not just *install* AI.


## What’s changing (and what’s not)

Rebranding touches everything, and yes—it’s a **pain**. By the time you read this, the **website has been updated** to match our new brand.

- **Changing:** Name, visuals, messaging—aligned to AI transformations as our core.  
- **Not changing:** Our standards for quality, our focus on outcomes, and our commitment to practical, business-grounded AI.

## The meaning we’re carrying forward

TransformAI is a promise: **we help you take control**. The brand is a reminder that the goal isn’t AI for AI’s sake—it’s AI that **fits your business**, your market, and your culture.
